# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** November 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how your information is handled when you use our application (the "Application").

## 1. Data Storage and Control

The Application is designed with a privacy-first approach. We do not operate external servers, databases, or cloud infrastructure to collect, store, or process your personal data.

All data generated, processed, or used within the Application remains strictly under your direct ownership and control. Data is stored through the following mechanisms only:

* **Local Storage:** All application data, configuration settings, and user-generated content are saved locally on your device.
* **Google Drive Integration (Optional):** If you choose to enable cloud sync or backup features, the Application may transmit data directly to your personal Google Drive account. This data is transferred securely between your device and Google's servers using official Google APIs. We do not have access to your Google Drive storage, your login credentials, or any files stored within your account.

## 2. Information Collection and Third-Party Access

* **No Personal Data Collection:** We do not collect, harvest, monitor, transmit, or sell any personal information, usage metrics, device identifiers, or analytics data.
* **No Third-Party Sharing:** Since we do not collect any data, no user data is ever sold, rented, or shared with third parties, advertisers, or analytics providers.

## 3. Third-Party Services

The Application utilizes Google APIs for authentication and optional Google Drive synchronization. Your use of Google services through the Application is governed by the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## 4. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this document.

## 5. Contact Information

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer via the official repository or project contact details.
