Constructor of the periodic account duration class, which needs mandatory arguments like starting date, time frequency and day of month and optional ones like the month of the year and the including start flag

  • _startingDate day to star the periodic account duration
  • _timeFrequency frequency to consider for signal the periodic duration
  • _dayOfMonth day of the month to signal
  • monthOfYear month of the year to signal (if it's annual of more)
  • includeStart flag to indicate if the start has to be signaled
